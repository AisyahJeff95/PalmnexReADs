Annual FFB Yield Calculation & Generation

Computes cumulative bunch counts, total FFB weight, Mean Bunch Weight (MBW), and extrapolates annual hectare yield based on planting density and recorded palm mortality.

Trial: DTP1
Estate: Teluk Intan
Density: 160 palms/ha
24 Harvested Bunches Sum of all round counts
404.9 kg Total FFB Weight Bunches + loose fruit
16.87 kg Mean Bunch Wt (MBW) Average kg / bunch
29.45 Annual FFB Yield Tons / Hectare / Year
Agronomic Calculation Formula (MPOB Standard):
Annual Yield = [ Total Plot FFB (kg) × Planting Density (palms/ha) ] / [ Bearing Palms Monitored × 1,000 ]
Generated annual dataset will be saved into DATAFFB.DBF for statistical ANOVA analysis.
