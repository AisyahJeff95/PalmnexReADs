Agronomic Research Architecture

Designed according to Palm Oil Research Institute of Malaysia (PORIM / MPOB) relational database specifications.

dBASE IV · SQL · HTML5/ES6
1. Census & Layout: Standardized factorial treatment blocks and individual recorded palms registered under unique plot coordinates.
2. Operational Rounds: Bi-weekly harvesting cycles and seasonal foliar sampling logged with full digital weighing validation.
3. Annual Plot Aggregates: Plot-by-plot synthesis combining productivity, palm vigor, foliar nutrition, and soil fertility over 12-month periods.
4. Biometric Exports: Immediate export to fixed-width ASCII matrices (*.DAT, *.PRN) formatted for SAS, GenStat, and Minitab ANOVA models.
